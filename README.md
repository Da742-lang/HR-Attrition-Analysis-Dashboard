# HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition to help HR teams understand why employees leave and what actions can improve retention. The analysis was performed using Power BI, leveraging data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visuals to uncover data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Identify key factors driving attrition  
- Analyze attrition across salary levels, age groups, job roles, and tenure  
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard  
- Provide actionable insights for HR decision-making  
- Improve data storytelling and reporting skills  

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary  
- **Total Employees:** 1416  
- **Total Attrition:** 229  
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.2%  
- **Average Age:** 37  
- **Average Salary:** 6.5K  
- **Average Tenure:** 7 Years  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  

### 1️⃣ **Attrition by Salary**  
- Salary ≤ **5K accounts for 70%** of total attrition (158 employees)  
- Low pay is the strongest contributor to employee turnover  

### 2️⃣ **Attrition by Age**  
- Highest exits: **26–35 age group (111 employees)**  
- Younger employees switch more due to growth & salary expectations  

### 3️⃣ **Attrition by Job Role**  
Top roles with highest attrition:  
- Laboratory Technician — 60  
- Sales Executive — 55  
- Research Scientist — 44  
- Sales Representative — 33  

These roles indicate high workload or lower compensation.

### 4️⃣ **Attrition by Tenure**  
- **57 employees left within their first year**  
- Shows onboarding issues or expectation mismatch

## 💡 Recommendations
